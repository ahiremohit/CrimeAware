About This Project

CrimeAware India

A data-driven platform for crime awareness, legal education, and citizen safety — built for India.

🎯 Mission

Millions of Indians are unaware of their basic legal rights, don't know what constitutes a crime, and hesitate to report incidents due to lack of knowledge. CrimeAware India bridges this gap by presenting NCRB crime data in a visual, interactive format alongside practical legal guidance every citizen needs.

📊 Data Source

Source: National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB), Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India Coverage: 28 States + 8 Union Territories — 2020, 2021, 2022 Dataset: IPC Cognizable Crimes — Total, Per-lakh rate, Charge-sheeting rate Use: Academic and public awareness purposes only. Not for commercial or legal proceedings.
